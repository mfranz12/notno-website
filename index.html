Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for real people who have too many accounts and too little patience for forgetting passwords.

🔐
Custom Encryption

Every password is encrypted using a custom cipher before it even reaches the database. Raw data is never stored.

📱
Multiple Accounts per Platform

Have two Facebook accounts? A main and a smurf on League? Store them all — labeled exactly how you want.

🏷️
Smart Labels

Tag accounts as Main, 2nd Account, Dummy, Alt, Smurf, Work, or any custom label. Find what you need instantly.

☁️
Cloud Sync

Your passwords live in the cloud via Firebase. Sign in from any device and your accounts are always there.

📊
Password Strength Analysis

See at a glance how many of your passwords are Strong, Medium, or Weak — and know which ones to fix.

🔑
Google Sign-In

Sign in with your Google account or email. Fast, secure, and no extra password to remember.

📋
One-tap Copy

Tap any field to copy username, email, or password directly to your clipboard. No typing, no mistakes.

🔄
OTA Updates

New features delivered automatically. The app notifies you when an update is ready — no Play Store needed.

How it works

Simple as 1, 2, 3.

Get set up in under a minute. No complicated setup, no confusing settings.

01
Download & Sign In

Download the APK, install it, and sign in with your Google account or create one with your email.

02
Add Your Accounts

Tap + to add any platform. Pick from 40+ known apps or type a custom one. Add as many accounts as you need.

03
Access Anytime

Tap any entry to reveal its details. Copy passwords with one tap. Everything is encrypted and synced to the cloud.

04
Stay Updated

New features roll out automatically. The app checks for updates on launch and prompts you when one is ready.
